The Studio

Design excellence, rooted in education.

Envisionar Design Atelier was founded by Prof. Bhuleshwar Mate, a retired Professor of Applied Arts and Design, Nagpur, with over fifty years in design education and practice.

The studio brings the rigour of academic design thinking to real-world commercial projects, from a startup's first logo to a corporation's exhibition pavilion.
