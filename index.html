05How we work

Simple, transparent, professional.

1

Brief & quote

Tell us what you need, by WhatsApp, call, or email. We respond within one working day with a clear scope and price range. No hidden costs.

2

Design & review

Prof. Mate personally directs every project. You receive concepts for review, with clear revision rounds included.

3

Delivery & support

Final files in every format you need: CorelDRAW masters, print-ready PDFs, Figma mockups. We stay available for print coordination.

  • CorelDRAW masters delivered, your printer can use them directly
  • Figma mockups so you approve before anything goes to print
  • Google Drive project folders, all files, always accessible
  • In-house 3D printing for custom artefacts and awards
  • WhatsApp-first communication, no tickets, no delays
