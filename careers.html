Junior Designer

Full-Time · Nagpur · Hybrid (remote after probation)

Envisionar Design Atelier is looking for a Junior Designer to join our small, hands-on studio — someone just starting out who wants to learn by doing, working directly across branding, print, signage, and packaging for real clients from day one.

Type
Full-time
Location
Nagpur (hybrid)
Compensation
Negotiable
Experience
0–2 years

What You’ll Actually Be Doing

  • Supporting brand identity work — logo exploration, layout, applying existing brand systems consistently across formats
  • Producing print-ready files for business cards, brochures, packaging, and signage
  • Building mockups and presentation decks for client pitches
  • Learning the full project workflow, brief to delivery, working directly alongside Prof. Bhuleshwar Mate

Who We’re Looking For

  • 0–2 years’ experience — fresh graduates welcome
  • A foundational grip on design principles (typography, colour, layout), paired with real enthusiasm to grow
  • Working knowledge of Figma, Adobe tools, and CorelDRAW
  • Comfortable with direct feedback and quick iteration — every project is personally reviewed before it goes to print or delivery

What You’ll Gain

  • Direct mentorship from a designer with fifty years in the field, on real commercial work
  • Broad exposure across brand, print, signage, and packaging rather than one narrow lane
  • Hybrid now, shifting to remote-friendly once you’re past probation

Sales & Outreach Assistant

Envisionar Design Atelier is seeking a part-time Sales & Outreach Assistant to drive sales of our published book Vernacular Branding, alongside design projects and a growing list of upcoming titles — flexible work suited to someone from a sales, marketing, or management background who wants meaningful work in publishing and design without a full-time commitment.

Type
Part-time, ~4–5 hrs/day
Location
Nagpur (field + remote)
Compensation
Negotiable + incentives
Start
Immediate

What You’ll Do

  • Promote and sell Vernacular Branding and forthcoming books to bookstores, design schools, institutions, colleges, universities, libraries, and individual buyers
  • Build and maintain relationships with retailers and institutional buyers across Nagpur and nearby cities
  • Manage online sales channels and coordinate order fulfilment and dispatch
  • Follow up on leads, enquiries, and bulk or institutional orders

  • Comfortable with WhatsApp Business, email, and spreadsheets
  • Own two-wheeler and smartphone preferred
  • Self-motivated, organized, and comfortable working independently
