04Using it

  • Give the logo clear space on every side, at least the height of the mark
  • Use the logo as supplied: do not recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects
  • Keep it on white or light grey so the blue mark stays visible
  • Write the name as Envisionar Design Atelier in running text
