The Envisionaridentity,explained.

The visual identity standards of Envisionar Design Atelier: logo, colour, typography and what not to do with them. Brand Guidelines v1.1, 2026, by Prof. Bhuleshwar Mate.

Two elements, one lockup: the icon mark and the wordmark.

Clear space

Clear space (a) = logo height ÷ 2

Allow breathing room around the logo. The clear space is half the height of the logo (a = h/2) on every side, and no other graphic element may enter it. Keep it consistent so the logo stays distinct and uncluttered.

The mark

The mark narrates the creative journey: thoughtful consideration, with the ultimate goal of enhancing other brands. It reflects the motto, envision enhance elevate. The intertwining of the ‘E’ and ‘N’ stands for:

Unity and integration

Ideas, design and execution brought together in one seamless approach.

Digital and physical

Translating digital concepts into the physical world, bridging both realms.

Brand purpose

The hug-like connection reflects our purpose: to elevate other brands, creatively and strategically.

04Don’ts

To protect the identity, these changes are not allowed. They apply to the icon, the wordmark and every brand mark without exception.

Rule 01Do not flip the logo

Never mirror or flip the logo or any part of it, horizontally or vertically.

Rule 02Do not apply unapproved colours

Only approved brand colours. No pink, green or custom tints outside the palette.

Rule 03Do not rotate the logo

It must always sit horizontal and level, at any angle of the layout.

Rule 04Do not stretch or distort

Always scale proportionally. Stretching breaks the visual balance.

Rule 05Do not change the arrangement

Do not reorder the elements or use lockups that are not approved.

Rule 06Do not apply effects

No drop shadows, glows, bevels, embosses or gradients directly on the logo mark.

The logo is never used on yellow.
