Vernacular Branding: book launch & micro-exhibition

A three-wall micro-exhibition marking the launch of Vernacular Branding, co-authored by Prof. Bhuleshwar Mate and architect Kaushambi Mate, walking visitors from the book’s open call and core concepts through to the studio’s own design history.

Held on 10 December 2025 at the Government College of Art and Design, Nagpur (formerly Chitrakala Mahavidyalaya), the micro-exhibition began as an open call and gathered graphic interventions and visual observations on identity, visibility and everyday aesthetics from contributors across India. More photographs are on vernacularbranding.in/exhibition ↗.
